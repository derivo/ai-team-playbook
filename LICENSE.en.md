## Attribution for playbook content

When sharing or adapting material published under `CC-BY-4.0`, attribution should
include at least:

- “AI Team Playbook contributors” as the creator attribution,
- a reference to <https://github.com/derivo/ai-team-playbook>,
- a reference to `CC-BY-4.0`, and
- an indication when changes were made.
